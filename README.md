**Golden Time** is a critically acclaimed romantic comedy anime series adapted from the light novels written by Yuyuko Takemiya. Set in a private law school in Tokyo, it explores college life, psychological struggles, and complex relationships.
---
## Main Characters
* **Banri Tada:** A fresh law student who suffered severe retrograde amnesia after falling off a bridge right after his high school graduation.
* **Koko Kaga:** An eccentric, beautiful, and intensely dramatic young woman who enters university to chase after her childhood friend Mitsuo.
* **Linda (Nana Hayashida):** A second-year student who shares a hidden connection to Banri's past life before his accident.

### Why Watch It?
1. **Unique Setting:** Unlike most romance anime set in high school, this takes place in college.
2. **Character Growth:** Features deep emotional arcs and realistic relationship hurdles.
3. **Memorable Soundtrack:** Includes iconic openings like "Golden Time" by Yui Horie.
